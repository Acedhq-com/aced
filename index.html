inside one run

The game and the studying are the same loop.

DRAW
  • Each round has a blind — BLINDyour round's score target.
  • Draw eight cards and play poker hands for chips × multiplier.
  • Spend winnings on jokers that bend the rules in your favor.
CONVICTION
  • Before banking a hand, bet LOW / MED / HIGH.
  • Read it right, it multiplies; misjudge it, you pay.
  • The self-read is fun even with no study content at all.
STUDY (WOVEN IN)
  • Open optional question practice between rounds.
  • Choose LOW / MED / HIGH confidence before submitting an answer.
  • Recorded answers and game progress describe different things.

why it works

NOT ANOTHER 'POINTS-ON-FLASHCARDS' APP.

  • Most study apps just put points, streaks, or badges on top of quizzes.
  • ACED starts as a game you'd play anyway — blinds, hands, jokers, and runs that can stand alone.
  • Your reviewed material is available in optional question practice.
  • Game rewards and progress are not proof of recall or future test performance.

ONE ENGINE, ANY SUBJECT

WHAT IT IS
ACED is a study-game engine, not a subject-specific app. Give it a set of questions or notes and it runs the same roguelike loop — hands, blinds, jokers, spaced review — on whatever you loaded.
WHAT YOU BRING
Supported structured pairs or authored questions, reviewed before play. The document importer can read text-based PDF and Word material; arbitrary articles and links are not automatically understood.
WHY A GAME
ACED combines a card game with optional question practice. Choose what to practice without treating the game score as evidence of recall.

YOUR NOTES STAY YOURS — HERE'S EXACTLY WHAT THAT MEANS.

NOTES PROCESSING

Pasted text and original PDF/Word files are processed in your browser. Supported structured material becomes study questions for you to review; ACED does not automatically understand arbitrary documents or fetch study links.

STORAGE & RETENTION

If you sign in and sync, study progress can include question, topic or source labels and other text-bearing study data. Optional launch updates send your email to the configured service. Older versions may already have retained email copies locally or in synced snapshots.

CONTROL & NO STREAK-GUILT

Deleting a pack removes that pack’s data in this browser. It does not clear every draft, earlier email copy, other device or cloud snapshot. Keep an export if you need a backup.
