Questions

Is this a free trial?

No. There is no trial, no tier, and no checkout anywhere in ACED. What you can play today is everything.

Then why does a pricing page exist?

Because people look for one before they invest time in a study tool. This is the answer: it costs nothing.

Is question practice required?

Play poker hands to clear score targets. Between rounds, optional question practice lets you choose LOW, MED or HIGH confidence. You can win the casual game without answering questions.

What can sync or launch updates send?

If you sign in and sync, study progress can include question, topic or source labels and other text-bearing study data. Optional launch updates send your email to the configured service. Older versions may already have retained email copies locally or in synced snapshots.

What does deleting a pack remove?

Deleting a pack removes that pack’s data in this browser. It does not clear every draft, earlier email copy, other device or cloud snapshot. Keep an export if you need a backup.
